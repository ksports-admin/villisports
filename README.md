# **🏆 Villisports - Live Regional Sports Updates Web App**

**Villisports** is an open-source web application built to power **live matchday coverage and scoring** for **grassroots-level sports tournaments** across India. Think of it like ESPNcricinfo, but not just for cricket — and entirely focused on **local, district, and state-level matches** from across the country.

## 📌 What is Villisports?

**Villisports** enables organizers to manage their tournaments digitally, while giving fans a platform to follow **live updates**, scores, and match schedules from games happening in their **preferred regions**.
From a kabaddi match in your village to a state-level football final — Villisports brings **local sports to the digital stage.**

## 🎯 Features

* 🏏 **Live Scores & Match Updates** – Real-time scoreboards for all supported sports.

* 📍 **Region-Based Discovery** – Users can choose their state and district to follow tournaments nearby.

* ⚽ **Multi-Sport Support** – Cricket, football, volleyball, kabaddi, and more.

* 📊 **Detailed Scoreboards & Stats** – Clean, mobile-friendly layouts for easy viewing.

* 🗓️ **Matchday Dashboard for Organizers** – Simple tools to schedule and manage matches.

* 🔔 **Follow Local Games** – Prioritized updates from users’ selected regions and important matches across India.

## 🌱 Why Villisports?

Grassroots sports in India are full of talent and passion — but lack digital visibility. Villisports bridges this gap by:

* Empowering local organizers to go digital

* Giving athletes recognition at the regional level

* Letting fans follow their hometown heroes live

## 🚀 Tech Stack (Planned)

* **Frontend**: Angular

* **Backend**: Laravel

* **Database**: MySQL / MongoDB

* **Real-time Updates**: WebSockets / Pusher

(Contributors are welcome to help implement and improve these layers.)
